Model Interpretability Guide
Statistical Note: Logistic Regression coefficients quantify statistical associations and directional log-odds impact within the training dataset. They do not establish direct causal relationships.
Primary Approval Drivers

Credit History Meets Guidelines (+1.70): Unquestionably the most influential positive predictor in consumer underwriting.
Semiurban Property (+0.46): Historically accompanied by the highest approval conversion rate.
Married Status (+0.27): Associated with increased household stability and dual-income potential.

Critical Risk Factors

Poor Credit History (-1.70): Delinquencies or lack of verified repayment record creates immediate downward pressure on approval.
Dependents > 0 (-0.37): Heightened household expenses reduce disposable debt service capacity.
Rural Collateral (-0.31): Appraisals in rural areas typically face liquidity constraints during collateral liquidation.
